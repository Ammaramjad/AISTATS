# Asset sources and licenses

The experience makes no external 3D asset or texture requests. The road network, buildings, trees, markers, and every vehicle are original procedural geometry authored in `src/lib/city-scene.ts` from Three.js primitives. They are covered by this repository's project license. No manufacturer trademarks, badges, or logos are used.

The interface loads **DM Sans** and **Playfair Display** from Google Fonts. Both are distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The system-font fallback keeps the booking flow usable if the font request fails.

Before production launch, an organization-level project license and font self-hosting policy should be added.
